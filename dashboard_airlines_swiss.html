Swiss International Air Lines (SWISS)

The flag carrier of Switzerland and a subsidiary of the Lufthansa Group. Operating from its primary hub at Zurich Airport (ZRH) and secondary base in Geneva (GVA), SWISS is renowned for Swiss precision, four-class intercontinental service, and being the global launch operator of the Airbus A220.

✈️

Cabin & Services

SWISS offers four distinct travel classes across its entire long-haul fleet with signature Swiss hospitality:

  • SWISS First & First Suites: Available on every long-haul widebody, featuring private suites, gourmet Swiss gastronomy, and dedicated First Class Lounges in Zurich.
  • SWISS Business: Lie-flat seating with direct aisle access options, staggered privacy, and the new "SWISS Senses" cabin design.
  • SWISS Premium Economy: Hard-shell seats with 39-inch pitch, wider cushions, and upgraded meal service on Boeing 777, A340, and A350 aircraft.
  • Miles & More & Swiss Chocolates: Integrated with the Lufthansa Group loyalty network, plus iconic complimentary SWISS chocolates served on every flight.
