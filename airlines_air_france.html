Air France

The flag carrier of France and a founding member of the SkyTeam alliance. Headquartered near Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG), Air France is renowned for French gastronomy, haute-couture service elegance, and an extensive global network spanning six continents.

Active Fleet Overview

Air France operates an all-Airbus short- and medium-haul fleet paired with modern Airbus A350, A330, Boeing 777, and Boeing 787 long-haul wide-bodies.

Cabin & Services

Air France showcases French culinary art and luxury travel across four long-haul cabin classes:

  • La Première (First Class): Exclusive four-suite cabin on select Boeing 777-300ER jets with floor-to-ceiling privacy curtains, Michelin-starred menus, and private Porsche tarmac transfers in Paris.
  • Business Class: Full-flat seats with direct aisle access and sliding privacy doors on retrofitted 777-300ER and new A350-900 aircraft.
  • Premium Economy: Dedicated cabin with extra-wide recliner seats, 38-inch pitch, and French-inspired multi-course meals.
  • Flying Blue & Air France Lounges: Shared loyalty program across Air France, KLM, and SkyTeam partners, with flagship lounges across CDG Terminals 2E, 2F, and 2G.

Major Hubs & Network

Connecting over 200 destinations across 85+ countries from its dual Parisian airport system:

  • Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) Primary Global Hub
  • Paris Orly (ORY) Domestic & French Overseas Hub
  • North America JFK, LAX, SFO, Atlanta, Miami, Montreal, Toronto
  • South America & Caribbean São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Buenos Aires, Pointe-à-Pitre
  • Africa & Asia-Pacific Abidjan, Dakar, Johannesburg, Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore
