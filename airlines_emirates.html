Emirates

Based in Dubai, Emirates is the world's largest international airline and the largest operator of both the Airbus A380 and Boeing 777. Operating exclusively wide-body aircraft, it connects six continents through its global super-hub at Dubai International Airport (DXB).

✈️

Active Wide-Body Fleet Overview

Emirates operates an all-wide-body passenger and cargo fleet, recently expanding with next-generation Airbus A350-900 deliveries alongside retrofitted A380s and 777s.

Cabin & Services

Renowned for luxury long-haul hospitality across four distinct cabin classes:

  • First Class Suites: Floor-to-ceiling enclosed suites on select 777-300ERs ("Game Changer") and private suites with onboard Shower Spas on the A380.
  • Business Class: Lie-flat seating, complimentary Chauffeur-drive service, and access to the signature A380 Onboard Lounge at 40,000 feet.
  • Premium Economy: Cream leather seats with 40-inch pitch, cushioned legrests, and elevated dining on A380, A350, and retrofitted 777 jets.
  • ice Inflight Entertainment & Skywards: Award-winning entertainment system with over 6,500 channels and the Emirates Skywards loyalty program.

Major Hubs & Network

Connecting over 140 destinations across 80+ countries and territories via Dubai:

  • Dubai International (DXB) — UAE Dedicated Terminal 3 Super-Hub
  • Dubai World Central (DWC) — UAE Emirates SkyCargo Hub
  • Europe & UK London Heathrow (6x daily A380), Paris, Frankfurt
  • Asia-Pacific & Australasia Sydney, Melbourne, Singapore, Tokyo, Bangkok
  • Americas & Africa New York JFK, LAX, São Paulo, Johannesburg
