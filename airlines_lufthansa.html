Deutsche Lufthansa AG

The flag carrier of Germany and one of Europe's largest airline groups. As a founding member of Star Alliance, Lufthansa operates an extensive global network centered around its primary dual-hub system in Frankfurt and Munich.

✈️

Cabin & Services

Lufthansa provides a four-class international travel experience, including its new Allegris cabin generation:

  • First Class & Allegris Suites: Private enclosed suites, caviar service, and dedicated First Class Terminal access in Frankfurt.
  • Business Class: Lie-flat seating with multiple seat configurations (including extra-long beds and privacy suites) on new A350s and 787s.
  • Premium Economy: Up to 50% more personal space, wider seats, and enhanced dining on all long-haul aircraft.
  • Miles & More: Europe's largest frequent flyer program shared across Lufthansa Group carriers (SWISS, Austrian, Brussels, Eurowings).
