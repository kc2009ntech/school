TUI Airways & TUI Group Airlines

The world's largest leisure and charter airline group, operating across five European carriers (UK, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, and Nordics). TUI connects regional European airports directly with holiday destinations across the Mediterranean, Caribbean, Americas, Africa, and Asia.

Cabin & Holiday Services

Tailored specifically for leisure travelers, package holidaymakers, and cruise connections:

  • Premium Club (Boeing 787): Dedicated front cabin featuring 38-inch seat pitch, wider seats, priority check-in/boarding, lounge access, and multi-course meals.
  • Extra Legroom & Space+: Available across both Boeing 737 and 787 fleets for added comfort on medium- and long-haul flights.
  • Fly-Cruise Integration: Seamless charter flights connecting regional UK and European airports directly with Marella Cruises and TUI Cruises ports.
  • Seatback Entertainment & Family Seating: Personal touchscreens on all Boeing 787 Dreamliners alongside dedicated family seating guarantees.
