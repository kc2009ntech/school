American Airlines

The world's largest airline by fleet size and scheduled passengers carried, headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas. Operating an extensive hub-and-spoke network centered at Dallas/Fort Worth (DFW), Charlotte (CLT), and Miami (MIA), American connects North America with Latin America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Cabin & Services

American Airlines offers tiered domestic and international cabins backed by global lounge access:

  • Flagship Business & Flagship Suites: Lie-flat seating with direct aisle access across all widebodies, featuring sliding privacy doors on new Boeing 787-9 and Airbus A321XLR aircraft.
  • Premium Economy: Wider leather seats with extendable legrests, enhanced dining, and priority airport privileges on international routes.
  • Main Cabin Extra & Wireless Streaming: Extra legroom seating, high-speed satellite Wi-Fi, and complimentary inflight entertainment streamed to personal devices or seatback screens.
  • AAdvantage & Flagship Lounges: The world's first airline loyalty program, offering Admirals Club and Flagship Lounge access across major global hubs.
