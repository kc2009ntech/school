Qatar Airways

The state-owned flag carrier of Qatar, headquartered in Doha. Operating a hub-and-spoke network from Hamad International Airport (DOH), Qatar Airways is a multi-time Skytrax Airline of the Year winner and the global launch customer for both the Airbus A350-900 and A350-1000.

✈️

Cabin & Services

Qatar Airways sets industry benchmarks for premium long-haul cabins and digital onboard connectivity:

  • First Class (A380): Ultra-wide lie-flat seats in a 1-2-1 layout with access to the upper-deck lounge and Al Safwa First Lounge in Doha.
  • Qsuite Business Class: Award-winning enclosed suites with sliding privacy doors, convertible double beds, and customizable 4-seat quad workspaces.
  • Oryx One & Starlink Wi-Fi: Up to 8,000 entertainment options paired with complimentary high-speed Starlink gate-to-gate connectivity across widebodies.
  • Privilege Club (Avios): Frequent flyer program utilizing Avios currency across Qatar Airways, British Airways, Iberia, and oneworld partners.
