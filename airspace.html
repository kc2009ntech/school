ICAO Standards

Understanding Global Airspace Classes

International civil aviation categorizes airspace into classes A through G to manage traffic density and ensure collision avoidance.

Class A - E (Controlled)

Requires ATC clearance and continuous two-way radio communication for all instrument flights.

Class F - G (Uncontrolled)

Flight information service provided with optional radio contact depending on local jurisdiction.
