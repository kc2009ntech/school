LATAM Airlines Group

Formed by the merger of Chile's LAN Airlines and Brazil's TAM Airlines, LATAM is the largest airline group in Latin America, operating an extensive network across South America, North America, Europe, and Oceania.

✈️

Cabin & Services

LATAM tailors its onboard experience across short-haul and intercontinental cabins:

  • Premium Business: Lie-flat seating with direct aisle access on Boeing 787 and 777 aircraft.
  • Premium Economy: Blocked middle seat, priority boarding, and extra legroom on regional A320 family flights.
  • LATAM Play: Seatback 4K screens on widebodies and wireless streaming on narrowbodies.
  • LATAM Pass: One of South America's largest frequent flyer loyalty programs.

Major Hubs & Network

Connecting over 145 destinations in 26 countries through key South American gateways:
