GOL Linhas Aéreas Inteligentes

One of Brazil's leading airlines and a pioneer of low-cost air travel in South America. Operating a standardized single-type Boeing 737 fleet, GOL connects major Brazilian hubs with domestic regional airports, South America, the Caribbean, and the United States.

✈️

Active Fleet Overview

GOL operates a standardized all-Boeing 737 passenger and dedicated freighter fleet, maximizing operational efficiency and rapid aircraft turnarounds.

  • Boeing 737-700 Next Generation
    Short-runway & regional domestic routes (including Rio Santos Dumont)
    138 Seats
  • Boeing 737-800 Next Generation
    Core Brazilian domestic & regional South American trunk routes
    186 Seats
  • Boeing 737 MAX 8
    Next-gen fuel-efficient flagship for domestic & international routes (Miami & Orlando)
    176–186 Seats
  • Boeing 737-800BCF (GOLLOG Cargo)
    Dedicated converted freighters operated for e-commerce & logistics
    Cargo Only
🛋️

Cabin & Services

GOL provides streamlined single-aisle comfort and digital connectivity across its domestic and international network:

  • GOL Premium Economy: Available on international flights, featuring a blocked middle seat, extra legroom, priority check-in/boarding, and upgraded catering.
  • GOL+ Conforto: Front-row and exit-row seats offering up to 34 inches of pitch and 50% more recline on domestic flights.
  • GOL Online Entertainment & Wi-Fi: Satellite Wi-Fi and live TV/streaming accessible directly on passengers' personal devices.
  • Smiles & GOL Lounges: One of Latin America's largest loyalty programs, paired with international and domestic VIP lounges in São Paulo (GRU) and Rio de Janeiro (GIG).
🌍

Major Hubs & Network

Connecting over 75 destinations across Brazil, South America, the Caribbean, and North America:

  • São Paulo Guarulhos (GRU) & Congonhas (CGH) Primary International & Domestic Hubs
  • Rio de Janeiro (GIG & SDU) Ponte Aérea & Coastal Hub
  • Brasília (BSB) & Fortaleza (FOR) Central & Northeast Connection Hubs
  • South America Buenos Aires, Montevideo, Santiago, Bogotá, Asunción
  • North America & Caribbean Miami, Orlando, Punta Cana, Cancún, Aruba
