TAP Air Portugal

The flag carrier of Portugal, headquartered at Lisbon Humberto Delgado Airport. TAP serves as a premier transatlantic bridge connecting Europe with Brazil, North America, and Africa via an all-Airbus mainline fleet.

✈️

Cabin & Services

TAP offers signature Portuguese hospitality and modern cabin products across its fleet:

  • Executive Class: Full-flat staggered Recaro beds on A330neo and Thompson Vantage seats on A321LR transatlantic flights.
  • EconomyXtra: Enhanced legroom (34-inch pitch), adjustable headrests, and power outlets on long-haul aircraft.
  • Portugal Stopover: Free stopover program allowing passengers to explore Lisbon or Porto for up to 10 days on connecting tickets.
  • TAP Miles&Go: Loyalty program integrated across all 25+ Star Alliance partner carriers.

Major Hubs & Network

Operating over 2,500 weekly flights to more than 85 destinations across 30+ countries:

  • Brazil Network (13 Cities) Largest European Carrier to Brazil
