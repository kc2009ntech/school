Air New Zealand

The flag carrier of New Zealand, headquartered in Auckland and recognized by its distinctive Koru tail emblem. Operating a hub at Auckland Airport (AKL), Air New Zealand connects domestic regional towns with Australia, the Pacific Islands, Asia, and North America.

✈️

Active Fleet Overview

Air New Zealand operates a streamlined fleet of Boeing 787 and 777 wide-bodies for ultra-long-haul routes, Airbus A320/A321 family jets for domestic and Tasman flights, and regional turboprops.

  • ATR 72-600
    High-frequency domestic regional trunk routes
    68 Seats
  • De Havilland Dash 8-300 (Q300)
    Regional provincial airport connector across North & South Islands
    50 Seats
  • Airbus A320-200
    Domestic main-trunk jet routes (Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch)
    171 Seats
  • Airbus A320neo

    165 Seats
  • Airbus A321neo (Domestic & International)

🛋️

Cabin & Services

Known globally for Kiwi hospitality, creative inflight safety videos, and innovative economy seating concepts:

  • Business Premier & Luxe: Lie-flat suites with memory foam mattresses, New Zealand wines, and new Business Premier Luxe suites on retrofitted/new 787s.
  • Premium Economy: Custom ink-colored leather seats with 41-inch pitch, legrests, and fine dining on Boeing 787 and 777 aircraft.
  • Economy Skycouch & Skynest: Pioneering Economy Skycouch (three-seat row converting into a flat couch) and Skynest bunk pods for ultra-long-haul flights.
  • Airpoints & Koru Lounge: Frequent flyer program with Airpoints Dollars and an extensive lounge network across New Zealand, Australia, and Fiji.
🌍

Major Hubs & Network

Serving over 20 domestic destinations across New Zealand and 30+ international gateways:

  • Auckland Airport (AKL) — North Island Primary International & Domestic Hub
  • Wellington (WLG) & Christchurch (CHC) Capital & South Island Hubs
  • Trans-Tasman (Australia) Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Gold Coast
  • North America (Non-Stop) New York JFK, Houston, LAX, SFO, Honolulu, Vancouver
  • Asia & Pacific Islands Tokyo Narita, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taipei, Nadi, Rarotonga
