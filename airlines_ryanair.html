Ryanair

Europe's largest low-cost airline group by passenger volume, headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. Operating across multiple European subsidiaries (Ryanair DAC, Ryanair UK, Malta Air, Buzz, and Lauda Europe), Ryanair connects hundreds of primary and regional airports across Europe and North Africa.

✈️

Active Group Fleet Overview

Ryanair Group operates nearly 650 aircraft—predominantly a standardized Boeing 737 single-aisle fleet alongside Airbus A320s flown by subsidiary Lauda Europe.

  • Boeing 737-800 Next Generation
    Core European short- and medium-haul point-to-point workhorse
    189 Seats
  • Boeing 737 MAX 8-200 ("Gamechanger")
    High-density next-gen narrowbody with lower fuel burn & quieter engines
    197 Seats
  • Airbus A320-200 (Lauda Europe)
    Operated by Vienna-based group subsidiary across Central & Southern Europe
    180 Seats
  • Boeing 737 MAX 10 (On Order)
    High-capacity next-generation fleet expansion (300 firm/option order)
    228 Seats
🛋️

Cabin & Services

Built around an ultra-low-cost, high-efficiency operating model with customizable fare bundles:

  • Standard Single-Class Cabin: Slimline non-reclining leather seats designed for rapid 25-minute airport turnarounds and high punctuality.
  • Priority & 2 Cabin Bags: Allows passengers to board first and bring both a small personal bag and a 10kg wheeled cabin bag onboard.
  • Flexi Plus & Plus Bundles: Includes reserved extra-legroom seats, Fast Track airport security, free airport check-in, and flexible ticket changes.
  • Getaway Café & Digital Boarding: Buy-on-board hot food, snacks, and duty-free retail paired with 100% digital app check-in.
🌍

Major Bases & Network

Operating from over 90 bases across more than 230 airports in 37 countries throughout Europe and North Africa:

  • London Stansted (STN) & Dublin (DUB) Largest Flagship Mega-Bases
  • Milan Bergamo (BGY) & Rome (FCO / CIA) Major Italian Hubs
  • Madrid (MAD), Barcelona (BCN) & Málaga (AGP) Key Spanish Bases
  • Brussels Charleroi (CRL) & Vienna (VIE) Central European Bases
  • North Africa & Mediterranean Marrakech, Agadir, Rabat, Faro, Porto, Tirana
