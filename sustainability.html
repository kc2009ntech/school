Industry Milestones

The Path to Net-Zero 2050

The commercial aviation industry has committed to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2050. Achieving this target requires synchronized efforts across fuel production, technological breakthroughs, and flight path optimization.

  • ✔️ 2030 Goal: 10% global SAF usage on commercial routes.
  • ✔️ 2035 Horizon: Regional hydrogen-powered passenger aircraft introduction.
  • ✔️ 2050 Target: Complete decarbonization across global air transport.
